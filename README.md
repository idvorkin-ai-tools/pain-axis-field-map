# The Pain Axis, mapped

A field-map explainer of **The Pain Axis: LLMs Represent Self-Directed Harm and Act on It** (Tagliabue, Dung, Berg, [arXiv:2609.16247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16247)).

Live: https://idvorkin-ai-tools.github.io/pain-axis-field-map/

- `index.html` — the spine: five arcs, ten part pages
- `parts/` — one page per section or topic of the paper (motivation, dataset, extraction, validation, self vs other, steering, the button task, what the choices track, discussion, limitations and appendices)
- `glossary.html` — 26 terms, from residual stream to re-press gap
- `paper.css`, `fig.js` — shared dark theme and click-to-enlarge figures

Original explanation written against arXiv v2 (25 Sep 2026), which the authors describe as ongoing work. Numbers are quoted from the paper with their section or figure. Method details were checked against the official code, [valen-research/Pain-axis](https://github.com/valen-research/Pain-axis) (MIT); the Figure 10 table in Part 08 uses that repository's source-derived rates, and Part 10 notes where the repository's [discrepancy list](https://github.com/valen-research/Pain-axis/blob/main/v2_controls/MANUSCRIPT_DISCREPANCIES.md) differs from the prose. Example sentences are from the MIT-licensed dataset release. The diagrams are this map's own. The paper is © its authors.

The paper studies representations and the behavior they can drive; it does not claim, and this map does not claim, that models feel pain.
